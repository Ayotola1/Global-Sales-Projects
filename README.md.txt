Global Sales Performance Analysis

📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes global sales data to understand revenue, profit, sales volume, ordering patterns, regional performance, and country-level performance.

The project was developed as a portfolio data analytics project using Power BI to transform sales data into interactive dashboards and actionable business insights.

The analysis is organized into:

- Page 1 — Financial Performance
- Page 2 — Sales & Order Performance


🎯 Business Objective

The analysis answers questions such as:

- Which region generated the most revenue?
- Which countries are the top five and bottom five based on profit?
- How does revenue by region change across the years?
- Which country generated the most revenue between 2011 and 2015?
- Which country ordered the most items, and what item did they order most?
- How do Fruits and Snacks compare in units sold?
- Which region and country were least profitable through the Online Sales Channel?
- Which Asian country generated the most profit from Beverages?

The project also translates findings into potential management actions.

🛠️ Tools Used

- Power BI — Dashboard development, visualization and interactive analysis
- DAX — Measures and calculations
- Power Query — Data preparation and transformation
- Excel/CSV — Source data and preliminary data handling

Objective

This dashboard focuses on ordering behaviour, product demand and country-level sales activity.

🧮 Analytical Approach

The analysis involved:

1. Reviewing the dataset structure.
2. Preparing fields for analysis and visualization.
3. Creating calculated measures where required.
4. Comparing revenue, profit and order performance across countries and regions.
5. Examining trends across years.
6. Comparing product performance.
7. Using interactive slicers to explore the data.
8. Translating findings into potential management actions.

💡 Key Insights

The dashboard identifies differences in:

- Revenue performance across regions with Europe as the highest performer.
- Profitability between countries, Kiribati ranking as the top profitable country.
- Country-level ordering performance by Lithuania.
- Product demand, including Fruits versus Snacks where Snacks exceeded Fruits.
- Online-channel profitability. 

🎯 Management Recommendations

1. Monitor order growth monthly to investigate low-performing countries where performance may be associated with customer demand, pricing, product availability, distribution coverage or competition.

2. Assess distribution coverage & ensure accessible distribution channels, in this instance review of Online Sales Channel performance.

3. Review product demand - Differences between product categories helps to inform inventory planning, promotions, product availability and market-specific strategies.

4. Increase marketing and brand awareness through targeted promotions - Study markets with strong revenue, profit or order volumes to identify practices that may be transferable to the bottom five countries.

5. Evaluate pricing competitiveness and improve pricing.
